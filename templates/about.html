About Pranika Bal Sewa

Pioneering professional, compassionate, and highly specialized pediatric physiotherapy in Nepal's Chitwan District.

Our Mission

At Pranika Bal Sewa Physiotherapy Center, we believe that every child deserves the physical bedrock to explore the world unhindered. Located in Bharatpur-10, our state-of-the-art facility aims to bridge the gap in specialized pediatric physical interventions within our community.

Our approach discards cold clinical environments in favor of play-based therapy. We harness a child's natural curiosity to build strength, correct gait patterns, and conquer neurological delays while they simply think they are having fun.
