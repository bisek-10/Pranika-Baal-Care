Our Physiotherapy Services

We combine evidence-based therapy with play-based intervention to help your child achieve optimal movement, independence, and a pain-free life.

Developmental Delay

Expert evaluation and stimulation for infants and toddlers not actively reaching their age-appropriate milestones.

  • Milestone stimulation (rolling, sitting, walking)
  • Torticollis & Plagiocephaly correction
  • Core strengthening and endurance

Neuro-Physiotherapy

Specialized management plans to increase functional capabilities for conditions impacting the central nervous system.

  • Cerebral Palsy (CP) management
  • Spina Bifida therapeutic approaches
  • Tone management & stretching protocols

Walking & Gait Analysis

Specialized correction for walking discomfort, "toe-walking," and uneven strides to ensure a steady walk.

  • In-toeing and out-toeing correction
  • Balance and stability training
  • Gait pattern re-education

Spine & Posture Care

Corrective exercises for spine alignment, scoliosis management, and rounded shoulders from heavy bags.

  • Scoliosis screening & management
  • Postural ergonomic advice
  • Strengthening spinal support muscles

Orthopedic & Musculoskeletal

Focused child-centric rehabilitation for injuries, abnormal bony growths, and post-operative recovery.

  • Flat feet, knock knees & bow legs
  • Sports injury recovery
  • Congenital condition management (CTEV)

Sensory Integration

Activities designed to assist kids who have difficulty interpreting sensory input safely in their environment.

  • Coordination & spatial awareness
  • Proprioceptive joint tracking
  • Gross & fine motor skill pairing

Chest & Respiratory Care

Non-invasive manual techniques to clear airway congestion and improve breathing efficiency in infants and children.

  • Mucus clearance & postural drainage
  • Breathing exercise education
  • Assistance for chronic asthma management

Post-Surgical Rehab

Comprehensive physical therapy programs to restore strength and range of motion after orthopedic or cardiac surgeries.

  • Scar tissue management & stretching
  • Progressive weight-bearing protocols
  • Safe return to sports/play programs
